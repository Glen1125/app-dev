# 🌄 The Lord of the Rings

> *"All we have to decide is what to do with the time that is given us."*  
> — Gandalf

## 🎬 Overview

**The Lord of the Rings** is a film trilogy directed by **Peter Jackson**, based on the novels by **J.R.R. Tolkien**. It includes:

1. *The Fellowship of the Ring* (2001)
2. *The Two Towers* (2002)
3. *The Return of the King* (2003)

---

## 🔑 Key Themes

- Courage in the face of darkness
- The burden of power
- Friendship and loyalty
- Hope beyond despair
